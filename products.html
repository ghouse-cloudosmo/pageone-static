Requirement Zapper

The task of scanning each and every single resume during the time when multiple applicants are applying for various jobs and selecting the best, which are either exact and a perfect match or nearly a closer match to the job specifications, has become a tedious, time-consuming and monotonous task. To lessen the overhead, we have a perfect solution for the recruiters. A tool, known as “Requirement Zapper”, is the solution to automating the e-mailing system about the requirements match to the seekers. This product would be helpful for various recruiters who regularly use web. The quality of this tool lies in RSS feed which allows the individuals and users to receive an updated information whenever any content on the website you are interested in, has been modified. The RSS feed is the technology which is setting current trends in the market by various recruitment agencies and other companies.

PageOne Systems, Inc uses this web based tool which has made the process of recruitment easier and efficient. The company will have the collection of resumes of the jobseekers in their database along with their contact information. Whenever there will be an RSS feed regarding the job requirements from the client’s companies or other different jobsites, this tool matches the job requirements with the job finders skills set and experience. Also the tool automatically crawls for the new job requirements and also searches for the new database. The matching individuals will be notified regarding the requirements via e-mail along with the company’s HR contact information.
