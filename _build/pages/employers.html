Hiring models

Choose the engagement that fits

Contract staffing / staff augmentation

Add skilled consultants to your team for a fixed term or project. Scale up or down as your needs change — without adding permanent headcount.

  • Fast start on urgent projects
  • Flexible duration
  • We handle payroll and administration

Contract-to-hire

Work with a consultant on the job first. When you’re confident in the fit, convert them to your own employee.

  • Lower hiring risk
  • Evaluate real performance
  • Smooth conversion process

Direct hire / permanent placement

We recruit for your permanent roles — sourcing, screening and presenting candidates who match your requirements and culture.

  • Targeted search in our specialty areas
  • Technical and cultural screening
  • Support through offer and onboarding

Dedicated project teams

Need more than one person? We assemble a complete team — for example data engineers, a DevOps engineer and a QA specialist — for your project.

  • Cross-skilled team from one partner
  • Single point of contact
  • Scales with your roadmap
