The problem

Manual matching doesn’t scale

When many applicants apply for many roles, reviewing every resume to find exact or close matches is tedious and time-consuming. Recruiters lose hours that should be spent talking to candidates.

Requirement Zapper is a web-based tool that automates this work, making recruitment faster and more efficient.

Matches in minutes, not days

Requirements in, best-fit candidates notified automatically.

How it works

Three steps, fully automated

01

Collect

Candidate resumes and contact details are stored in a central database.

02

Monitor

The tool follows RSS feeds from client companies and job sites, automatically picking up new requirements.

03

Match & notify

Requirements are matched against each candidate’s skills and experience, and matching candidates are emailed with the hiring contact’s details.
